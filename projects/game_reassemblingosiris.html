Saving the Transcript

At the start of the game(or any time via the “save transcript” command/button), you will be prompted to begin recording a transcript. Press SPACE to continue, enter a filename (the date is usually good), and click Save. You will see a confirmation once recording has started.

Downloading Your Transcript

To download your transcript, click Files in the left sidebar, and you will see your transcript listed under Transcripts. We would love if you can send that to us through the feedback form above!
