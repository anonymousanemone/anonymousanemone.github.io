Introduction

This is a digitized version of Gardiner's Sign-list, meant to look and navigate like the PDF (but loads faster and is searchable). It is one page, and can be searched with cmd/ctrl + F. Page numbers are annotated with each entry, and linked to the PDF page.

Signs can be searched by unicode or their Gardiner number. Sign entries are formatted as "D36" (with no space in between the letter and number), while signs referenced in descriptions is formatted as "D 36" (with a space).

The hieroglyphs are in the process of being manually typed up, and are rendered with Djehuty (see credits) for stacking glyphs. The glyphs will be completely searchable in reading order. You can download the most recent version of the JSON file used to render this webpage from Github.
